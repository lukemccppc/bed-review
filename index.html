Buying guide

What to Know Before You Buy

The four things that actually decide whether a mattress will feel right to you.

Mattress Type

Memory Foam
Excellent pressure relief and motion isolation. Great for side sleepers and couples, but can sleep warmer and feel harder to move around on.
Hybrid
Foam plus coils. Better airflow, edge support, and bounce. Often the safest, most broadly appealing construction.
Latex
Responsive, durable, and naturally breathable, but usually pricier, with a distinctive springy feel.
Innerspring
Traditional and bouncy with strong airflow, but generally less contouring.

Firmness

1–3Soft
4–5Med-Soft
6–7Med-Firm
8–10Firm

Firmness isn't absolute. Lighter sleepers usually experience a mattress as firmer than its rating, while heavier sleepers sink in more and feel it as softer. Most people land comfortably in the 5–7 range.

Sleeping Position

Side sleepers
Prioritize pressure relief around the shoulders and hips.
Back sleepers
Need balanced support with moderate contouring.
Stomach sleepers
Need firmer support so the hips don't sink out of alignment.
Combination sleepers
Want a responsive surface that's easy to change positions on.

Body Weight

A 130-lb side sleeper and a 240-lb stomach sleeper can have completely different experiences on the exact same mattress.

Under 130 lbs
Mattresses feel firmer. Softer, contouring foam usually feels best.
130–230 lbs
Most medium to medium-firm mattresses work well.
Over 230 lbs
Look for coil support, strong edges, and durable materials to prevent sagging.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I spend on a good mattress?

Most people can get an excellent queen mattress for $800–$1,600. Below about $600, materials and durability tend to drop off. Above $2,000, you're usually paying for luxury features rather than meaningfully better sleep.

What mattress firmness is best?

Medium-firm (around 6–7 out of 10) suits the widest range of sleepers. Side sleepers and lighter people often prefer a bit softer, while stomach sleepers and heavier people usually do better a bit firmer.

What's the best mattress for side sleepers?

Of our top three, Serta is the best fit for side sleepers thanks to its deep, pressure-relieving foam around the shoulders and hips.

Are hybrid mattresses better than memory foam?

Neither is better for everyone. Hybrids offer more bounce, airflow, and edge support. Memory foam offers more contouring and motion isolation. Hot sleepers and heavier sleepers often prefer hybrids, while side sleepers and couples often love foam.

How long should a mattress last?

A quality mattress typically lasts 7–10 years. Hybrids and latex often last toward the longer end, while lower-density foams may wear sooner, especially under heavier body weights.

When should I replace my mattress?

Replace it if you see visible sagging, wake up stiff or sore, sleep better away from home, or the mattress is more than 8–10 years old.

Are mattresses in a box any good?

Yes. Many of today's best mattresses ship compressed in a box. They expand fully within 24–72 hours and perform just as well as mattresses delivered flat.

Should I buy a mattress online?

Online is often cheaper and comes with long at-home trials, which tell you far more than lying on a showroom bed for five minutes. Just check the return policy before you buy.

What happens if I don't like my mattress?

All three of our picks offer an at-home sleep trial. If it isn't right, you can typically return it within the trial window. Give it at least 30 nights first, since your body needs time to adjust.

Do I need a box spring?

Usually not. Most modern foam and hybrid mattresses work on any solid or slatted foundation, a platform bed, or an adjustable base. Check each brand's warranty for its specific support requirements.
