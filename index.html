Our #1 pick, explained

Why Tuft & Needle Earned Our #1 Spot

Tuft & Needle doesn't win every category. It's the brand that gets the most important things right for the widest range of sleepers.

01

Responsive, Not Sink-In Foam

Every T&N mattress uses its own T&N Adaptive® foam. Traditional memory foam slowly molds around you and is slow to spring back. Adaptive foam cushions you but responds right away, so it's easy to roll over and you don't feel "stuck" in the bed.

Tuft & Needle says Adaptive® foam has 35% more responsiveness than memory foam.
02

Built to Sleep Cooler

All-foam beds can trap heat. T&N's Adaptive® foam is more breathable than standard memory foam and is infused with cooling gel and heat-conducting graphite to pull warmth away from your body. Current models also add a cooling cover.

Tuft & Needle says it has 25% more airflow than memory foam.
03

Balanced Pressure Relief

The comfort layer softens pressure at the shoulders and hips. A firmer support foam (and coils, in the hybrids) keeps your spine aligned underneath. The result is cushioning without the hammock-like sag that can cause morning stiffness.

04

Straightforward Value

Instead of dozens of near-identical models with confusing names, Tuft & Needle keeps a short, clear lineup. Each step up adds a feature you can understand, like gel memory foam or coils, and prices stay well below luxury brands.

Is Tuft & Needle Right for You?

No mattress is right for everyone. Here's an honest look at who will love it and who should look elsewhere.

Tuft & Needle Is a Great Fit If…

  • You prefer balanced medium to medium-firm comfort
  • You dislike the deep, stuck-in-foam feeling
  • You change positions throughout the night
  • You tend to sleep hot
  • You want strong value without luxury-mattress pricing
  • You want a relatively simple mattress-buying experience

You May Prefer Something Else If…

  • You want an extremely plush, pillowy mattress
  • You love deep, slow-sinking memory foam
  • You want an ultra-firm, traditional innerspring feel
  • You regularly sit or sleep right at the edge of the bed

For a deeper hug and more pressure relief, consider Serta. For maximum edge support and a classic coil feel, consider Beautyrest.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tuft & Needle a good mattress brand?

Yes. It's our #1 overall pick for its responsive, cooler-sleeping foam, simple lineup and strong value. It isn't ideal for everyone, though. People who want a very plush feel or maximum edge support may prefer Serta or Beautyrest.

Which Tuft & Needle mattress should I buy?

As a general guide: the Original for back and stomach sleepers or tighter budgets, the Mint for side sleepers and hot sleepers who want more pressure relief, and the Original Hybrid for couples and heavier sleepers who want coil support.

How much should I spend on a good mattress?

Many people can get an excellent queen mattress for roughly $700–$1,600. Much below that, materials and durability tend to drop off. Well above $2,000, you're usually paying for luxury features rather than meaningfully better sleep.

What's the best mattress for side sleepers?

Of our top three brands, Serta offers the deepest pressure relief for side sleepers. If you prefer Tuft & Needle's more responsive feel, the T&N Mint adds gel memory foam for extra shoulder and hip cushioning.

Are hybrid mattresses better than memory foam?

Neither is better for everyone. Hybrids offer more bounce, airflow and edge support. Foam offers more contouring and motion isolation. Hot sleepers and heavier sleepers often prefer hybrids, while side sleepers often love foam.

How long should a mattress last?

A quality mattress typically lasts 7–10 years. Hybrids often last toward the longer end, while lower-density foams may wear sooner, especially under heavier body weights.

Are mattresses in a box any good?

Yes. Many of today's best mattresses, including every Tuft & Needle model, ship compressed in a box. They expand within a day or two and perform just as well as mattresses delivered flat.

What happens if I don't like my mattress?

All three of our picks offer a 100-night trial when you buy from the brand directly. If it isn't right, you can return it within that window. Give it about 30 nights first, since your body needs time to adjust.

Do I need a box spring?

Usually not. Most modern foam and hybrid mattresses work on any solid or slatted foundation, a platform bed, or an adjustable base. Check each brand's warranty for its specific support requirements.
