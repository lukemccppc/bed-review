FAQ

What firmness is best for most sleepers?

Medium to medium-firm suits the widest range of people. Side sleepers and lighter people often prefer a bit softer; stomach sleepers and heavier people usually do better a bit firmer.

Which Tuft & Needle mattress should I choose?

The Original for back and stomach sleepers or tighter budgets, the Mint for side and hot sleepers who want more pressure relief, and the Original Hybrid for couples and heavier sleepers who want coil support.

Is Tuft & Needle good for side sleepers?

Yes, especially the T&N Mint, which adds gel memory foam for shoulder and hip relief. If you want an even deeper, plusher hug, Serta is our top pick for side sleepers.

How does the sleep trial work?

Buy directly from tuftandneedle.com and you get 100 nights from delivery. If it isn't right, start a return online for a full refund; T&N arranges free pickup, and most returns are donated. Limit one return per household every 12 months.

Is Tuft & Needle worth the price?

For most shoppers, yes. Queens start at $699, well below premium brands, and you still get responsive, cooler-sleeping foam, a 10-year limited warranty and free shipping and returns.
